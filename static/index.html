Transfer Policy 2009 (Memo 291-AR & AH)

Statutory Rules & Criteria for ARD Department Transfers & Promotions

Automated Transfer Decision Compliance Engine

Every single promotion, transfer, and service utilization (SU) in this board is evaluated dynamically in real time against the following 6 core statutory criteria:

1. Area Tenure Norms 5 yrs / 4 yrs

Standard maximum tenure in a station/district is 5.0 years for general areas, and 4.0 years for difficult and hill districts (Darjeeling, Kalimpong, Alipurduar, Cooch Behar, Jalpaiguri, Uttar Dinajpur, Dakshin Dinajpur, Jhargram, Purulia). Officers exceeding this norm receive a 🔴 RED rotation alert.

2. Children Academic Board Exam Safeguard Para 13

Under Memo 291 Para 13, officers whose children are appearing in Board Examinations (Class X Madhyamik/ICSE/CBSE or Class XII HS) must NOT be displaced outside their current district during the academic session. A 🔴 RED warning is triggered if breached.

3. Superannuation Exemption DOR ≤ 2 Years

Officers within 2 years of retirement date (DOR) are exempted from compulsory transfers and entitled to choice of home district or convenient station under statutory retirement welfare norms.

4. Working Spouse Co-location Para 5

Under Memo 291 Para 5, when an officer's spouse is employed in Government service or recognized educational institutions, postings should be accommodated in the same district or contiguous stations.

5. Home District Restriction Rule 75

Officers in administrative posts (Deputy Director, Assistant Director HQ) are generally prohibited from being posted to their Home District, unless within 2 years of superannuation.

6. 50-Point Roster Reservation Quota WBCSC Statute

The candidate's community category (SC, ST, OBC-A, OBC-B, UR) must align strictly with the statutory reservation cycle of the 50-Point Roster point.
